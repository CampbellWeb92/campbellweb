Why Do You Need a Website?

Having a website offers many advantages for individuals, businesses, organizations, and professionals. It helps build trust, increase visibility, and create more opportunities online.


01

Establishes Credibility

A website helps people find and trust you, making your business look more legitimate and professional.

02

Available 24/7

Your website is accessible anytime, allowing visitors to learn about your products or services from anywhere.

03

Expands Your Reach

A website helps you reach people beyond your local area through search engines and social media.

04

Cost-Effective Marketing

Showcase products, publish articles, collect leads, and promote special offers affordably.

05

Improves Customer Service

Use FAQs, contact forms, chat features, and information pages to answer common questions quickly.

06

Increases Sales Opportunities

Generate leads, accept bookings, process payments, and promote related products or services.

  • .co.za domain renewal: approximately R150–R250 per year. Other domain extensions may cost more.

Helpful Information

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about pricing, turnaround times, hosting, content and ongoing support.

How long does a website take?

Most websites can be completed within 1–7 days. Larger websites and projects requiring extensive content may take longer.

Is hosting and domain registration included?

Yes. The listed website packages include one year of domain registration and hosting. From the second year, estimated renewal costs are R150–R250 per year for a .co.za domain and R600–R1,500 per year for website hosting, depending on the website’s requirements, usage, and hosting plan. Exact renewal costs are always confirmed before renewal.

Do I need to supply the text and images?

Clients should supply their logo, business information, service details, prices and preferred images. Guidance can be provided on organising the content.

Will my website work on mobile phones?

Yes. Every website is designed to adapt to phones, tablets and desktop screens.

Can you update my website later?

Yes. Minor updates, ongoing maintenance and larger improvements can be quoted according to the work required.

How many revisions are included?

Reasonable revisions are completed during the review stage within the agreed project scope. Major changes or new features may require a separate quote.

Can you add WhatsApp, Google Maps or enquiry forms?

Yes. These features can be included where suitable for the selected package and your business requirements.

What payment options are available?

You can choose a once-off payment, a short payment plan or an eligible monthly website package. A deposit is required before work begins on once-off payments and short payment plans. Monthly packages are billed monthly and can be cancelled at any time.
